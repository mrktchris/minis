**Your AI agents fight in your taskbar.**

Taskbar Fighters gives every AI coding tool on your computer a pixel-art fighter: Claude Code, Codex, Gemini, Cursor,
Ollama, LM Studio, Copilot CLI and more. Two fighters at a time stand in the empty stretch of the Windows 11 taskbar (on a
Mac, on top of the Dock), and what they do follows what your agents are really doing. They spar while both agents are
idle, clash while one is working, and hand over when a task finishes. When an agent hits its context limit, its fighter
gets knocked out.

This is the first tagged release.

The Windows zip is not code-signed, so SmartScreen may ask once: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Run one copy at a
time: the zip, a pip install and a git checkout share one data folder, so `stop` one before starting another.

every push, and it has run on the author's Mac, but few Macs have tried it. If it draws in the wrong place, run
`taskbar-fighters doctor` and paste the report into an issue
([docs/MAC-TESTING.md](https://github.com/mrktchris/taskbar-fighters/blob/v1.0.0/docs/MAC-TESTING.md)).

## Highlights

- **Auto-detect.** Claude Code and Codex are read from their local session files (numbers only). About a dozen other
  tools are found in the process list. No hooks or config edits, and nothing is installed into your agents.
- **13 armed fighters in HD.** 128 px art by default (`size 96` or `size 64` for smaller). Each program gets its own
  fighter, and six extras can be worn by any program: Kunoichi, Knight, Barbarian, Monk, Nomad and Oni.
- **Every fighter fights like its weapon.** 8-10 weapon moves per character on top of the shared set: 3-hit strings, air
  juggles, throws, chain pulls, dash-through cuts, guard breaks, ripostes and weapon locks, with sparks per hit kind.
- **Locked on, with real jumps.** Fighters always face and aim at each other, even after a dash-through swaps their sides.
  Jumps have a crouch, a rise, a hang at the apex and a landing.
- **Cinematic set pieces.** Hand-directed exchanges for both fighters (a blade lock, a cross-cut freeze, an aerial finish
  and more) play at a match's dramatic moments.
- **Battles from real usage.** Best-of-3 matches with HP bars, supers, a signature ultimate per fighter, impact frames and
  a K.O. finisher.
- **Arena mode.** The fight breaks out onto a whole monitor: window edges become walls and ledges, glass cracks and
  shatters, beams clash. `arena --rounds 1|3|5|endless --intensity calm|normal|wild`. **Visual only: nothing real happens
  to your windows.**
- **Desktop weapons.** In the arena, a fighter cuts a piece out of a window, it shatters into pixels and re-forms as a
  blade, axe, spear, club or shards in the fighter's hand, then cracks with every hit and breaks. See Privacy below.
- **Scoreboard, HUD plates, fighter select, tray icon** (menu-bar icon on a Mac), and a flash toggle for anyone who
  dislikes strobing.

## Privacy

- It reads numbers and states only: token counts, timestamps and tool-call states. No prompts, replies, window titles or
  file names. It makes no network calls and never edits your AI tools' settings.
- The arena never touches your windows. It reads window rectangles, never titles, and paints only on its own overlay.
- **Desktop weapons grab your screen into memory.** When an arena starts it takes one picture of its monitor into RAM to
  forge weapons from. It is never written to disk, a log or a temp file, and it is dropped when the arena closes. **Run
  `forge off` before you share your screen** (`taskbar-fighters forge off`, or untick **Desktop weapons** in the tray
  menu). The taskbar fighters never read the screen. On macOS the picture needs the Screen Recording permission, which the
  app never asks for; without it the weapons are made from window shapes.

Details: [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/mrktchris/taskbar-fighters/blob/v1.0.0/SECURITY.md) and the README's
[Privacy and safety](https://github.com/mrktchris/taskbar-fighters/blob/v1.0.0/README.md#privacy-and-safety).

## Known limits

- Windows: the taskbar must be at the bottom; only 100% DPI on the primary display has been seen. The default fight zone
  was measured on one 2560x1440 display; other layouts may need a `zone` in `minis.json`.
- macOS is experimental. Desktop weapons from real pixels, the arena's CPU use, the notch badge, the new jumps and
  lock-on, a side or auto-hiding Dock, two displays and Intel Macs have not been checked on a real Mac yet.
- At the default 128 px a fighter stands above the taskbar, over the bottom of the desktop (click-through except the
  sprite); `size 64` keeps it close to the taskbar.
- The arena aims for 30 fps and drops itself to 20 fps when it cannot keep up (it does on the author's PC).
- Programs found in the process list have no context numbers, and their "working" state is a CPU heuristic.
- The art is generated by a rig from pose data, not hand-drawn.
